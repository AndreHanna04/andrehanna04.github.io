About

Hello there

I'm Andre Hanna, a graduate game developer based in Beverly Hills, NSW, specialising in Unreal Engine 5. I studied Game Design at the Academy of Interactive Technology (2023–2025), building a broad skill set across programming, technical art, and level design.

My work spans Unreal C++ systems, advanced materials, 3D character creation, and cinematic animation — I love understanding how all the pieces fit together. I've built multiplayer shooters, PS5-ported games, and complex open-world systems. My philosophy: if you do it, do it different.

Outside of game dev, I spend time critically analysing games, sketching character designs, and experimenting with stylized shaders. A 6-month QA internship at Sweaty Chair sharpened my eye for detail and gave me a strong appreciation for polish.
